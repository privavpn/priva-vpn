Priva VPN — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Priva VPN ("the app", "we") is a WireGuard-based VPN client. This policy explains what the app accesses and how data is handled. Your privacy is the point of this app, so we keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the service.

Data stored on your device

Your VPN configuration — including private keys and server address — is stored only on your device, in encrypted form. It is never sent to us. It is used solely to establish the encrypted tunnel to the server you connect to.

Data processed to provide the service

What we do not do

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no trackers. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

Permissions

The app requests the VPN permission to create the tunnel, and notification permission to show connection status. These are used only for those purposes.
